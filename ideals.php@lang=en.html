The Core Values

The principles that define why the party exists and how it acts.

Home › The Core Values
01

Value 1

Social consciousness without caste

A society organised around shared responsibility rather than inherited hierarchy.

02

Value 2

Reverence for linguistic diversity

Every language carries a people's memory, and each deserves standing.

03

Value 3

Protection of our traditions and culture

Carrying Telugu tradition forward — and, in Australia, passing it to the next generation.

04

Value 4

Nationalism without neglecting regional aspirations

Pride in the nation that begins with, rather than erases, the region.

05

Value 5

Relentless fight against corruption

No accommodation with corruption, at any level and in any form.

06

Value 6

Progress that preserves the environment

Development that leaves the land, water and air fit for those who come next.
