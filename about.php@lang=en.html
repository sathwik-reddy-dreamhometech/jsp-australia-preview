About Us

Serving the community, together

Janasena Melbourne Community Association Inc. is a non-profit organisation established in Melbourne to unite Telugu families, students, professionals, and community members. Our inspiration comes from the values and principles upheld in the Telugu States (Andhra & Telangana) — especially the spirit of integrity, service, discipline, and social responsibility championed by Janasena.

We proudly operate as a community-focused organisation, and we are associated with TAAI (Telugu Association of Australia Inc.), strengthening our cultural and community reach across Victoria.

Our purpose is simple: build unity, serve the community, promote values, and celebrate Telugu identity.

Our Vision

To build a respectful, transparent, and service-driven Telugu community organisation in Melbourne that stands for unity, culture, and meaningful contribution.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and families through community service, cultural education, welfare initiatives, and leadership development.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, inclusiveness, service to humanity, cultural pride, and collaboration.

Our Commitment

We are committed to non-profit operations, transparent financial management, community-first decision making, and the promotion of Telugu culture and service-oriented values.

Join Us

Whether you are a student, professional, family, or new migrant — you are welcome. Together, we can build a stronger, more connected Telugu community in Melbourne.
