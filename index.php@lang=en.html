About Us

Serving the community, together

Janasena Australia is a group of individuals across Australia who support the ideals of Janasena — social justice, transparency and service.

Founded in 2014 by Sri Pawan Kalyan, the party's guiding principle is that lasting change comes from ordinary people acting together.
