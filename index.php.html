About Us

Serving the community, together

Janasena Melbourne Community Association Inc. is a non-profit organisation bringing together Telugu families, students, professionals and community members across Melbourne.

Our purpose is simple: build unity, serve the community, promote values, and celebrate Telugu identity — inspired by the spirit of integrity, service and social responsibility championed by Janasena.
